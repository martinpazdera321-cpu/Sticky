# 📝 Sticky

**Sticky** is a small macOS app that lives in your notch.
Click the 🧠 icon in the menu bar and a black panel slides out from under the notch with three buttons:

- **📝 Sticky note:** a paper note in your choice of 4 colors. It stays on your screen above every app, on every desktop, and even after a restart. The only way to get rid of it is to throw it away: it crumples into a paper ball and flies into the bin.
- **🛡️ Shield:** watches your Downloads folder and warns you about suspicious files (fake double extensions, unsigned apps, scripts).
- **🧠 Settings:** sticky note color, open at login, app info.

Small details: real notch shape, paper crumple animation and sounds, a soft click sound with trackpad haptics.

Built with SwiftUI + AppKit by Martin Pazdera.
